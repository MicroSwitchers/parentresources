Story Tap

Parents can process their own personalised 5-10 sentence stories into an audio book, or voice output tool for their child. The app is on rails, so children can tap through the pages independently like a cause and effect toy, as it reads the book in high-quality audio. The app processes everything on your phone or tablet, never sending anything off your device, and it's free—this has only become possible in recent months. The caveat is it may take up to 15-45 minutes on some phones to initially process the story. When that is done, it can be played over and over. The app has a host of visual access options, as well as auditory and haptic cues.

Runs on the free, open source community project Kokoro TTS.

Use Story Tap

Touch Deck

A customisable large button app with up to 4 colourful buttons that can speak phrases or play recorded audio when tapped. Buttons can be repositioned, resized, and colour-coded. Each button supports up to 3 rotating phrases with text-to-speech or your own voice recordings. Includes haptic and auditory feedback options, activation cooldown settings, and full-screen mode. Perfect for simple communication, choice-making, or as a cause and effect activity.

Use Touch Deck

GazeCanvas v2

Version 2 of GazeCanvas is an image canvas designed to be efficiently manage visual targets like images, video, shapes and text for use with a gaze tracking device overlay. Animation can be applied to the targets and triggered by pressing the numbers on a standard keyboard. This requires a gaze tracking device with its own overlay software.

Key Features:

  • Media Library
  • Fast image loading
  • Auto-Load Folders
  • Include Movement
  • Add Text
  • Quick Background Colours
  • Foreground and Background Images
  • Create Complexity Images

Note: This app is designed for rapid setup and experimentation. It does not save arrays between sessions.

Use GazeCanvas v2

About These Projects

Privacy Note: These apps process data locally. They do not send any information outside of your browser. None of the apps will ask you to input any identifying information or context. For example, age calculators will never allow the user to input anything other than an arbitrary date, disconnected from any context.
